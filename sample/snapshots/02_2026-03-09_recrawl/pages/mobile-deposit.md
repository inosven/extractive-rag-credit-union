# Mobile Check Deposit

Deposit a check from anywhere with Maple Mobile.

## How to deposit a check

1. Sign the back of the check and write "For mobile deposit only at Maple Ridge CU".
2. In Maple Mobile, tap Deposit, then choose the account.
3. Enter the amount and take a photo of the front and the back of the check.
4. Tap Submit. Keep the check for 14 days, then destroy it.

Deposits made before 4 p.m. on a business day are usually available the next business day. Daily deposit limits may apply; contact us for your limit.
