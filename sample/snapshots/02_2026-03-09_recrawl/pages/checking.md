# Everyday Checking

Everyday Checking is a free checking account with no monthly service fee and no minimum balance.

## What you get

- A free Maple Ridge debit card
- Free online and mobile banking with bill pay
- Overdraft protection from your Share Savings

## How to open

You can open Everyday Checking online in about ten minutes or at any branch. You need a government-issued photo ID, your Social Security number and an opening deposit of $25.

## Who can join

Membership is open to anyone who lives or works in Harlow County or Pine County. A one-time $5 deposit in a Share Savings account makes you a member-owner.
