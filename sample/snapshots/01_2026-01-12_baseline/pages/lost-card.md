# Lost or Stolen Card

## What to do right away

If your debit or credit card is lost or stolen, call us right away at 555-014-2200. After hours, call the 24-hour card line at 555-014-2299.

You can also lock your card yourself. In Maple Mobile, go to Cards, choose the card and tap Lock Card. Unlock it the same way if you find it.

## Getting a new card

We will cancel the old card and order a new one. A replacement card usually arrives in 7 to 10 business days. A replacement fee may apply; see the fee schedule.
