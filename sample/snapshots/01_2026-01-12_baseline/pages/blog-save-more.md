# Five small ways to save more

Saving is easier when it is automatic.

1. Set up a recurring transfer on payday.
2. Round up your debit card purchases into savings.
3. Put a windfall, like a tax refund, into a certificate. When this article was written in 2024, a 12 Month Certificate paid 4.75% APY.
4. Review your subscriptions twice a year.
5. Give each goal its own savings account.
